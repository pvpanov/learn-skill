S1. Concept, as the question it answers for you

Why this matters for you. The decision in the owner's own work that this concept changes, posed as the question they would actually ask, so that the reason to read on is clear before any definition, notation or formula appears.

The running example. One small concrete case from the owner's world, with actual numbers, introduced here before any display formula, and small enough that every later step can be worked through on it by hand.

The first piece of theory: set up the problem, define each term before using it, say in words why each step follows from the previous one (no formal proof unless asked), and after every formula say what it means in the running example.

The takeaway for your decision. What the owner should do or choose differently now that the conclusion has been reached by the reasoning above, stated in terms of the same example and their own work.
